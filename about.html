About Me

Hi there, and welcome! You can call me Solar Song.

Professionally, I’m a full-stack AI engineer. I build SaaS web apps for car manufacturers and AI workflows for accounting and audit teams. But I think of myself as a polymath, because my curiosity reaches well beyond code. I’m drawn to music production, game modding, and hands-on DIY projects.

Music has been part of my life for over a decade. I play guitar and drums, and I’m comfortable on keyboard too, which turns out to be essential for music production. You can hear some of the pieces I’ve written over the years on my Instagram:fortunecookie555

I also love building things in the physical world. Recently I designed my own air purifier with a personalized three-layer filter that captures PM2.5, PM10, formaldehyde, and dust. I’m still testing it and will spend another month running it alongside an air quality monitor, making adjustments as I go. Once that’s done, I plan to write a blog post about the process.

I like to think of myself as smart and a little lazy. I look for the simplest solution that works. That mindset led me to switch my development environment from Windows and macOS to Linux, and I’ve spent a lot of time tuning it to fit my habits. I’d love to share what I’ve done with Fedora 44 to boost productivity someday, so keep an eye out for that post too.

Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to look around, and say hello if something here catches your interest!

Found a typo or have a question? Feel free to send me an email or reach out on GitHub.
